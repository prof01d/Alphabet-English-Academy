Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 14-day trial work?
You get full access to the platform for 14 days without any charges. If you don't cancel before the trial ends, you'll be automatically enrolled in your selected plan.
Can I upgrade or downgrade later?
Absolutely! You can change your plan at any time through your account settings. Changes are applied at the start of your next billing cycle.
What happens if I miss a group class?
Recorded sessions are available for most group classes. However, live attendance is encouraged for the best learning experience and interaction.
What happens if I miss a group class?
Premium members can book 1-on-1 sessions with our certified tutors via the dashboard. You can schedule them at any time that fits your schedule, subject to availability.
