THE FOUNDATION

A Vision Born from Global Experience

Founded in 2012 by a collective of Oxford and Cambridge linguists, Alphabet English Academy started with a simple observation: traditional language schools were failing to keep pace with the demands of a digital, globalized economy.

We realized that fluency isn't just about grammar—it's about confidence, cultural context, and the ability to articulate complex ideas. Our journey began in a small London office and has since expanded to serve students in over 40 countries.

Today, our mission remains unchanged: to empower every student with the linguistic tools they need to transcend borders and achieve their full professional potential.
